    Workshop in Dialectology

    TERRA HOMINIS – 2026

    The Dialectological Study Group of the School of Linguistics at HSE University invites submissions for a workshop in dialectology to be held within the 4th Student and Doctoral Conference “TERRA HOMINIS – 2026”.

    Abstracts are invited in two areas: (i) Dialectology of the East Slavic languages, (ii) Digital methods in dialectology.

    The first area is intended for any research on the phonetics, grammar and lexicon of dialects of the East Slavic languages. Preference is given to corpus-based studies and to work drawing on field data. In their talks, speakers are encouraged to devote particular attention to methods of data collection and processing and to research methodology. Discussion of the sociolinguistic challenges that a dialectologist faces in the field is likewise welcome.

    The second area focuses on research concerned with the use of corpus data, methods of dialect classification, automatic processing of dialect materials, and the creation of resources for dialectological research. We invite discussion of experience with both East Slavic dialects and dialects of other languages of the world.

    Students and doctoral students are invited to participate. To apply, please fill in the form on the conference website: https://hum.hse.ru/terrahominis-2026/application. In the “section” field, please select “Theoretical and Typological Linguistics”, and state the workshop area in your abstract. The abstract should be between 200 and 400 words (excluding references).

    Submission deadline: 15 February 2026 (inclusive)

    For all enquiries, please write to: dialectologicalstudygroup@gmail.com
