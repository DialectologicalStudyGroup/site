    Completed Projects

    Database of the Dialectological Atlas of the Russian Language

    The Dialectological Atlas of the Russian Language (DARYA) is a fundamental linguogeographic study carried out at the Institute of the Russian Language of the USSR Academy of Sciences (now the Russian Academy of Sciences) in the 1970s–1990s. It covers the territory of the early settlement of East Slavic tribes (up to the end of the 16th century), where the main dialects of the Russian language took shape over a long period. The materials for compiling the atlas were collected in 1946–1966 according to a special Programme (https://www.ruslang.ru/doc/dialectolog/voprosnik_atlas.pdf), which contains 294 questions on dialect phenomena in the fields of phonetics, morphology, syntax, and vocabulary. The database we have created contains, in electronic form, material on linguistic features recorded in 4,196 settlements, which you can explore on the interactive map. The website allows users to obtain complete information on the linguistic parameters of a single settlement on a single page, as well as to generate a map of the distribution of one or more features. Phonetic, morphological, syntactic, and lexical maps are available in the database.

    Projects in Development

    Bank of Dialectological Maps

    The plan is to create a bank of electronic maps of various dialectological phenomena that have been mapped by different researchers but remain difficult to access and poorly legible due to print quality. The work involves several stages: collecting a library on linguogeography, scanning maps, creating a unified base map for these maps, and developing tools for aligning these maps within a single cartographic system. The first batch of material will be the volumes of “Vostochnoslavyanskie izoglossy” (East Slavic Isoglosses). These are 50 maps devoted to various dialect phenomena in the territory of the East Slavic dialects, printed in the 1990s. The scientific significance of these maps and the descriptions accompanying them is undeniable and has been explored in a large number of works. However, the print quality of the maps makes reading them extremely difficult and creates ambiguous areas for interpretation. Recreating these maps using modern cartographic methods is undoubtedly an important task for Russian dialectology and linguogeography.

    Electronic Regional Dialectological Atlases

    An electronic resource containing regional atlases of various dialect phenomena is currently being developed and regularly updated. At present, atlases of Bashkiria, Chuvashia and Mari El, as well as Volgograd, Moscow, and Arkhangelsk Oblasts, are already available to users. The resource allows users both to examine individual dialect features and to combine them for the analysis of bundles of isoglosses.
