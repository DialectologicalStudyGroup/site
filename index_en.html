Dialectological Circle

Who we are

We are a group of linguists working in Russian dialectology and in the dialectology of the East Slavic languages. We share an interest in fieldwork on Russian dialects and in their history, in bringing dialect data to bear on the typological picture of language, and in organising data on Russian dialects in a form suited to contemporary scholarship. The core of the group consists of students and faculty of the School of Linguistics at HSE University, although the circle also includes researchers from other institutions.

Why a “circle”?

In calling ourselves a circle we wish to stress that our existence is independent of external circumstances and institutions.

What we do

The grammar and phonetics of Russian dialects, dialectometry, areal research, the compilation of linguistic maps, databases of Russian dialects, and dialect corpora.

News

A new themed issue of the journal Rhema, devoted to research by the Dialectological Circle, has been published

The issue comprises nine articles covering various levels of language, from phonetics and prosody to morphosyntax and word formation. The papers presented are the outcome of five years of expeditions by the Dialectological Circle (2021–2025), in which students, doctoral students and faculty of the School of Linguistics took part. The full contents of the issue are available on the journal’s website.
